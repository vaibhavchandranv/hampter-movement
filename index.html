what is the hampter movement?

the hampter movement is a community-driven initiative focused on raising awareness about educational inequality across south asia. whether through tutoring, mentoring, volunteering, resource creation, outreach, or simply spreading awareness, every contribution helps.

share your hampter contributions

volunteered? taught a class? created notes? donated books? mentored students? share a screenshot, photo, or description of your contribution and inspire another hampter to help.
